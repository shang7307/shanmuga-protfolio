SHT 02/10 — ABOUT

About Me

The bridge between hardware and software.

I'm Shanmugapriyan J, a Mechatronics Engineering student at Coimbatore Institute of Engineering and Technology. My interest sits right at the intersection of mechanical systems, electronics, and code — I like understanding how a machine senses, decides, and acts, and then building the parts that make that possible.

My work spans embedded systems, robotics, and mechanical assembly on one side, and automation, AI workflows, and software development on the other. I enjoy taking an idea from a rough sketch to a working prototype — wiring up a microcontroller, building an RPA bot that removes a repetitive task, or shipping a small web app end to end.

I stay sharp by showing up: RPA workshops, generative AI buildathons, hackathons, and hands-on engineering competitions like the E-Kart Championship. I'm now looking for an internship or in-plant training role where I can apply that foundation to real problems and contribute from day one.
